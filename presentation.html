M Vendor enablement
05 · Trust & eligibility

Structured onboarding

Clear tiers.
Progressive trust.

Vendor participation is tied to an active plan and tier-specific checks. Tier 2 verification is reviewed before its badge and higher financial limits apply.

Tier 1 · Basic

$5 / month

  • Monthly Paystack plan
  • Basic identity and bank soft-check
  • 10% vendor commission
Tier 2 · Verified

$10 / month

  • Monthly Paystack plan
  • Identity document reviewed by admin
  • 5% commission after approval

Subscriptions are charged in NGN at the operator-configured USD/NGN rate. Identity checks are not a substitute for government-record validation.

M Protected checkout
07 · Payment flow

Escrow makes the handoff explicit

Payment is a journey—not a blind transfer.

Buyers can check out across eligible vendors in one order. The backend confirms price, quantity, and stock before funds are locked.

A · AUTHORIZE

Confirm order

Use current server prices and verified stock.

B · PROTECT

Hold in escrow

Payment stays held until the delivery outcome.

C · RELEASE

Settle fairly

Receipt confirmation releases vendor proceeds.

2.5%Buyer escrow fee
10% / 5%Tier 1 / approved Tier 2 commission
20%Of positive price uplift recorded as surge share

A refund decision is recorded for reconciliation; gateway refunds still require completion in Paystack.

Price and stock are confirmed by the server—not trusted from the browser.
